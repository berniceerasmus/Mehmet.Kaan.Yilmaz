Section 2 — Grammar

Articles: a / an / the

When to use A and ANIndefinite Article
Rules in English

Use a before consonant sounds: a shirt, a laptop, a book.

Use an before vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u): an umbrella, an orange, an app.

Use a / an when you mention something for the first time, or when it is not specific — you do not know which exact one, or it doesn't matter which one.

Formula: Subject + verb + a / an + noun
→ I need a charger. (any charger will do)
Türkçe Açıklama

a ünsüz sesle başlayan kelimeler önünde kullanılır: a shirt (bir gömlek).

an sesli harfle (a, e, i, o, u) başlayan kelimeler önünde kullanılır: an umbrella.

Türkçede "a / an" yerine "bir" kullanılır ya da hiç kullanılmaz. İlk kez bahsedilen veya belirsiz bir şey için kullanılır.

Kural: Belirsiz, herhangi biri = a / an
→ I want a jacket. (herhangi bir ceket)
I want a shirt. She needs an umbrella. He has a laptop. I need an adapter.
When to use THEDefinite Article
Rules in English

Use the when the listener already knows which one you mean.

Use the when something has been mentioned before.

Use the with unique things: the sun, the Pacific Ocean, the internet.

Use the with superlatives: the best, the largest.

Pattern (first → second mention):
I bought a laptop. The laptop is fast.
Türkçe Açıklama

the belirli bir şey için kullanılır; Türkçede doğrudan karşılığı yoktur.

Daha önce bahsedilen bir şey tekrar söylendiğinde the kullanılır.

Dünyada tek olan şeyler için kullanılır: the sun (güneş), the Pacific Ocean.

Kural:
İlk söyleyişte = a. Sonraki söyleyişte = the.
→ I have a book. The book is interesting.
The shirt is blue. We ship across the Pacific Ocean. The fitting room is on the left. This is the best laptop.
When NOT to use an article (Ø zero article)Zero Article
Rules in English

No article before plural nouns used in general: Books are expensive.

No article before uncountable nouns in general: Furniture costs money.

No article before proper names of most countries and cities: Turkey, Ankara, London.

Türkçe Açıklama

Genel anlamda çoğul isimler önünde article kullanılmaz: Books are expensive.

Sayılamayan isimler genel anlamda kullanıldığında article gerekmez: Furniture costs money.

Çoğu ülke ve şehir ismi önünde article kullanılmaz: Turkey, Ankara.

Ø Books are expensive. Ø Furniture costs a lot. She lives in Ø Turkey.

Section 3 — Grammar

Quantifiers: some / any / a lot of / lots of

SOME — positive sentences & offersPositive
Rules in English

Use some in affirmative (positive) sentences with countable plural nouns and uncountable nouns.

It means "a certain number" — you don't give the exact amount.

Also use some in questions when you expect a "yes" answer or when making an offer or request.

Formula: Subject + verb + some + noun
→ I have some books.  |  Can I have some water?
Türkçe Açıklama

some olumlu cümlelerde kullanılır. Türkçede "bazı", "biraz" ya da hiç çevrilmez.

Sayılabilen çoğul ve sayılamayan isimlerle kullanılır.

Teklif veya nazik ricada da kullanılabilir: Would you like some help?

Kural: Olumlu cümle = some
→ I have some chairs. (Birkaç sandalyem var.)
I have some books. We have some furniture. Would you like some help? She bought some clothes.
ANY — negatives & genuine questionsNegative / Question
Rules in English

Use any in negative sentences and genuine questions where you don't know the answer.

Negative: "I don't have any chairs" = the quantity is zero.

Question: "Do you have any chairs?" = you are genuinely asking.

Negative: Subject + don't/doesn't have + any + noun
Question: Do/Does + subject + have + any + noun?
Türkçe Açıklama

any olumsuz cümlelerde ve gerçek sorularda kullanılır. Türkçede "hiç" ya da çevrilmez.

Olumsuzda: I don't have any phones. (Hiç telefonum yok.)

Soruda: Do you have any jackets? (Ceketiniz var mı?)

Kural: Olumsuz & soru = any
→ We don't have any beds. / Do you have any beds?
Do you have any chairs? I don't have any money. Do you sell any laptops? We don't have any sofas left.
A LOT OF / LOTS OF — large quantityLarge Quantity
Rules in English

A lot of and lots of mean the same thing: a large quantity.

Both work with countable and uncountable nouns.

Used mainly in positive sentences, but can appear in questions too. Lots of is slightly more informal.

Formula: Subject + verb + a lot of / lots of + noun
→ We sell a lot of products.
Türkçe Açıklama

a lot of ve lots of ikisi de "çok" anlamına gelir ve birbirinin yerine kullanılabilir.

Hem sayılabilen hem sayılamayan isimlerle kullanılır.

Genellikle olumlu cümlelerde kullanılır; lots of daha gayri resmidir.

Kural: Büyük miktar = a lot of / lots of
→ She has lots of clothes. (Çok kıyafeti var.)
We sell a lot of products. She has lots of clothes. There are a lot of customers today.

Section 4 — Grammar

Do / Does Questions & Negatives

Forming Yes / No QuestionsPresent Simple Questions
Rules in English

In present simple, use do or does to form questions.

Use do with: I, you, we, they

Use does with: he, she, it

Important: The main verb stays in its base form (no -s) when you use does.

Question formula:
Do / Does + subject + base verb + object?
→ Does she sell phones? (NOT: Does she sells)
Türkçe Açıklama

İngilizce geniş zamanda soru kurmak için cümlenin başına do veya does getirilir.

do → I, you, we, they ile kullanılır

does → he, she, it ile kullanılır

Önemli: does kullanıldığında ana fiil -s almaz, sade kalır.

Soru kalıbı:
Do / Does + özne + yalın fiil?
→ Does he work here? (NOT: Does he works)
Do you sell phones? Does she work here? Do they have any laptops? Does he sell furniture? Do we have any chargers?
Negative sentences: Don't / Doesn'tPresent Simple Negatives
Rules in English

To make a negative, add don't (= do not) or doesn't (= does not) before the main verb.

Use don't with: I, you, we, they

Use doesn't with: he, she, it

Negative formula:
Subject + don't / doesn't + base verb + object
→ She doesn't sell clothes. / I don't have any beds.
Türkçe Açıklama

Olumsuz cümle için don't veya doesn't kullanılır.

don't → I, you, we, they

doesn't → he, she, it

Olumsuz kalıp:
Özne + don't / doesn't + yalın fiil
→ He doesn't have a laptop. / I don't sell books.
I don't have any chairs. She doesn't work here. They don't sell furniture. He doesn't have a phone.

Dialogues · Diyaloglar

Dialogue 1 — In a clothing storeGiyim mağazasında
Customer
Good morning. I'm looking for a jacket. Do you have any in size L?
Günaydın. Bir ceket arıyorum. L bedeninde var mı?
Assistant
Good morning! Yes, we have some jackets in size L. Would you like a blue one or a black one?
Günaydın! Evet, L bedeninde birkaç ceket var. Mavi mi yoksa siyah mı istersiniz?
Customer
I prefer the black one. How much does it cost?
Siyahı tercih ederim. Fiyatı ne kadar?
Assistant
The black jacket is 250 lira. But we have a discount on it this week — it's only 200 lira.
Siyah ceket 250 lira. Ama bu hafta indirimde, sadece 200 liraya.
Customer
That's great. Can I try it on?
Harika. Deneyebilir miyim?
Assistant
Of course! The fitting room is at the back, on the right.
Tabii ki! Soyunma kabini arkada, sağda.
Customer
It fits perfectly. I'll take it. Can I pay by card?
Tam oldu. Alıyorum. Kartla ödeyebilir miyim?
Assistant
Yes, of course. Shall I also show you our new shirts? They go well with this jacket.
Evet, tabii. Yeni gömleklerimizi de göstereyim mi? Bu ceketle çok iyi gider.
Customer
Why not! I also need a shirt for work. Do you have any in white, size L?
Neden olmasın! İş için gömlek de lazım. L bedeninde beyaz var mı?
Assistant
Yes, we have some on the second shelf. Here is your receipt. Thank you and have a nice day!
Evet, ikinci rafta birkaç tane var. İşte fişiniz. Teşekkürler, iyi günler!
Dialogue 2 — In an electronics storeElektronik mağazasında
Customer
Excuse me, do you sell laptops here?
Affedersiniz, burada laptop satıyor musunuz?
Assistant
Yes, we have a lot of laptops. What do you need it for — work or games?
Evet, çok sayıda laptopumuz var. Ne için lazım — iş mi oyun mu?
Customer
It's for work. I need a laptop with a big screen and a long battery life.
İş için. Geniş ekranlı ve uzun pil ömürlü bir laptop istiyorum.
Assistant
I recommend this model. The screen is 15 inches and the battery lasts about 10 hours.
Bu modeli tavsiye ederim. Ekranı 15 inç ve pili yaklaşık 10 saat dayanıyor.
Customer
Does it come with a charger?
Şarj aleti ile mi geliyor?
Assistant
Yes, it includes a charger and a cable. We also sell headphones and a laptop bag.
Evet, şarj aleti ve kablo dahil. Ayrıca kulaklık ve laptop çantası da satıyoruz.
Customer
Do you have any headphones on sale?
İndirimde kulaklık var mı?
Assistant
Yes, we have some wireless headphones at a very good price today. Would you like to see them?
Evet, bugün çok iyi fiyatlı kablosuz kulaklıklarımız var. Görmek ister misiniz?
Customer
Yes, please. Do they work with this laptop?
Evet, lütfen. Bu laptopla uyumlu mu?
Assistant
Yes, they connect via Bluetooth, so they work with any laptop or phone.
Evet, Bluetooth ile bağlanıyor, yani her laptop veya telefonla çalışır.
Customer
Perfect. I'll take the laptop and the headphones. Here is my card. Can I get a receipt?
Mükemmel. Laptop ve kulaklığı alıyorum. İşte kartım. Fiş alabilir miyim?
Assistant
Of course! Great choice. Enjoy your new laptop and headphones!
Tabii ki! Harika seçim. Yeni laptopunuzun ve kulaklığınızın tadını çıkarın!

Yes, we have it in white, grey, and walnut brown. Which colour do you prefer?

Section 7

Reading · Okuma

Read the text carefully · Metni dikkatlice okuyun

A Global Company
GlobalMart is a large international company. It sells many different kinds of products around the world. The company ships products across the Pacific Ocean, the Indian Ocean, and the Atlantic Ocean. It has warehouses in more than thirty countries and delivers to customers in over eighty countries. Last year, GlobalMart sent more than five million packages to customers worldwide.

What does GlobalMart sell?
GlobalMart sells furniture, electronics, clothes, and books. In the furniture section, there are a lot of tables, chairs, sofas, beds, and shelves. The electronics section is very popular — customers can find phones, laptops, tablets, chargers, cables, and headphones. The clothing section has dresses, jackets, shirts, pants, and shoes in many different sizes and colours. The bookstore sells novels, dictionaries, and magazines from authors all over the world.

The customer service team
GlobalMart has a large and friendly customer service team. The team works every day, from Monday to Sunday, from 8 in the morning to 10 at night. Customers can call the team, send an email, or use the online chat on the website. The team speaks English, Turkish, Arabic, and several other languages. If a customer does not like a product, they can return it within thirty days and get a full refund. The company gives a receipt for every purchase.

Shopping at GlobalMart
Shopping at GlobalMart is easy and enjoyable. Customers can visit a physical store or shop online from any device. In the store, professional shop assistants always help customers find what they need. A customer can ask: "Do you have this in size M?" or "Does this laptop come with a charger?" The assistants are always friendly and knowledgeable. If there is a discount on a product, the price tag clearly shows the old price and the new, lower price. GlobalMart offers a lot of seasonal discounts, especially in summer and winter.

Payments and delivery
Customers can pay with cash or a credit card in store. The company also accepts all major online payment methods. Delivery is fast — most products arrive in two to five working days. Some products qualify for free delivery. If a customer orders furniture, the delivery team can also help with assembly. GlobalMart is one of the most trusted shopping companies in the region, and it has a lot of loyal customers who shop there regularly.

Turkish translation · Türkçe çeviri

Küresel Bir Şirket: GlobalMart, dünya genelinde birçok farklı ürün satan büyük bir uluslararası şirkettir. Pasifik, Hint ve Atlantik Okyanusları boyunca ürün göndermektedir. Otuzdan fazla ülkede depoları olan şirket, seksenin üzerinde ülkedeki müşterilere teslimat yapmaktadır. Geçen yıl dünya genelinde beş milyondan fazla paket gönderilmiştir.

Ne satıyor? Mobilya, elektronik, giyim ve kitap satılmaktadır. Mobilya bölümünde çok sayıda masa, sandalye, kanepe, yatak ve raf bulunur. Elektronik bölümünde telefon, laptop, tablet, şarj aleti, kablo ve kulaklık mevcuttur. Giyim bölümünde farklı beden ve renklerde elbise, ceket, gömlek, pantolon ve ayakkabı satılmaktadır.

Müşteri hizmetleri haftanın yedi günü, sabah 8'den gece 10'a kadar çalışmaktadır. Müşteriler telefon, e-posta veya çevrimiçi sohbet yoluyla ulaşabilir. Ürün beğenilmezse otuz gün içinde tam iade yapılabilmektedir.

Alışveriş mağazadan veya çevrimiçi yapılabilir. Profesyonel görevliler her zaman yardımcıdır. Nakit veya kredi kartıyla ödeme yapılabilir; çevrimiçi ödeme de kabul edilmektedir. Çoğu ürün iki ila beş iş günü içinde teslim edilmektedir. GlobalMart, bölgenin en güvenilir alışveriş şirketlerinden biridir.
